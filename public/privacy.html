Privacy Policy – Noor Quran

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Noor Quran ("the app") is developed by Aamir Kapadia. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, translation, reciter, tafsir, theme, font size), bookmarks and last-read position are saved only in your device's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Third-party content services

To show Quran text, translations, tafsir, audio and hadith, the app requests public content from api.alquran.cloud, cdn.islamic.network, everyayah.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net and fonts.googleapis.com. As with any internet request, these servers may receive technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not send them any personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If features that use personal data are added (for example, location for prayer times), we will update this policy before releasing them.
