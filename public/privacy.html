Privacy Policy – Quran Safar

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Quran Safar ("the app") is developed by Aamir Kapadia. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no cross-site tracking.

Anonymous usage counts

To understand which features are used, the app sends anonymous, aggregate page and event counts (for example “prayer screen opened” or “app installed”) to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service. GoatCounter uses no cookies and stores no personal data: no names, no accounts, no precise location, and no IP addresses (these are only used briefly to count unique visits and are not stored). Counts are only viewable in aggregate. If your browser or device sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is sent. Your Quran bookmarks, notes, trackers and settings never leave your device.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, translation, reciter, tafsir, theme, font size), bookmarks and last-read position are saved only in your device's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Third-party content services

To show Quran text, translations, tafsir, audio and hadith, the app requests public content from api.alquran.cloud, cdn.islamic.network, everyayah.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net. The app's fonts are bundled with the app and are not requested from any server. As with any internet request, these servers may receive technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not send them any personal data.

Location (prayer times, Qibla and nearby mosques)

If you tap “Use my location”, your device's location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction and to show your nearest city name (from a city list built into the app), and is saved only in local storage. To find nearby mosques or show a place name, the approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim / Overpass) under their own privacy policies. You can instead choose a city from the list and no location is used.

Microphone (Quran Live and Qaida Live)

The microphone is used only when you start Quran Live or Qaida Live. Audio is processed on your device and is not recorded, uploaded or stored.

Notifications

If you turn on prayer notifications, the schedule is calculated and stored on your device and shown by your browser. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Contact
