Privacy Policy – Quran Safar

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Quran Safar ("the app") is developed by Aamir Kapadia. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no cross-site tracking.

Anonymous usage counts

To understand which features are used, the app sends anonymous, aggregate page and event counts (for example “prayer screen opened” or “app installed”) to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service. GoatCounter uses no cookies and stores no personal data: no names, no accounts, no precise location, and no IP addresses (these are only used briefly to count unique visits and are not stored). Counts are only viewable in aggregate. If your browser or device sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is sent. Your Quran bookmarks, notes, trackers and settings never leave your device.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, translation, reciter, tafsir, theme, font size), bookmarks and last-read position are saved only in your device's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Third-party content services

To show Quran text, translations, tafsir, audio and hadith, the app requests public content from api.alquran.cloud, cdn.islamic.network, everyayah.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net. As with any internet request, these servers may receive technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not send them any personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If features that use personal data are added (for example, location for prayer times), we will update this policy before releasing them.
