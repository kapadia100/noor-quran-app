Install on iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (or in the ••• menu).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add (top right). Open Quran Safar from your Home Screen.

Already installed an older version? Delete the old icon first (press and hold the icon, then Remove App), then add it again.
